New subpath `@aikitstudio/agul-react/effects/auto-animate`: with `effects={[autoAnimateEffects()]}`, cards and chat messages slide into place when items are added, removed or reordered (optional peer `@formkit/auto-animate` ^0.10). Plugin drivers can now implement `list`; `useListChanges(region)` returns the container props.
