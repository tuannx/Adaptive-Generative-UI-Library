Presentation for read-only data. `@aikitstudio/agul-core` adds `resolveDisplay` / `formatDisplayValue` / `statusTone` (money, percent, numbers, dates, emails, links, images, status tags from `format`, field name, value or `x-agul-display`) and `planPresentation` / `planRows` / `planRecord` (KPI stats, card grid, timeline, table, record; pin with `x-agul-present`). `@aikitstudio/agul-react` formats list cells, detail and action results with them, shows KPI cards for numeric records, and lets lists switch to a card grid or timeline when the rows suit it (table stays the default).
